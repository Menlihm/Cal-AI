# TrenTree

Daily nutrition companion for **healthy weight loss**: calorie & macro targets, vitamin/mineral checklists, gentle eat reminders, photo check-ins, and menstrual-cycle-aware food ideas.

## Features

- **Personalized calories** via Mifflin–St Jeor BMR × activity, with safe floors (1200♀ / 1500♂)
- **Macros, fiber, water** targets for your goal pace
- **Daily vitamins & minerals** using sex- and age-aware RDA-style targets
- **Eat reminders** in-app (+ optional browser notifications) when you’ve gone too long without a bite
- **Photo check-ins** with optional weight notes (stored locally)
- **Male / female** profiles; **cycle tracking** with phase-based craving suggestions grounded in common nutrition research themes
- Data stays **on your device** (localStorage)

## Note

TrenTree is educational self-tracking — not medical advice. Cycle food ideas synthesize common themes from micronutrient RDAs and menstrual-nutrition education; individual needs vary.
